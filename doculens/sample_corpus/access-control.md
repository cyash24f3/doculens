# Access & permissions

Fictional Atlas documentation for DocuLens evaluation. This is not a real service policy.

## Roles

The four workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer. Owners manage billing and can delete the workspace. Admins manage members and workspace settings but cannot delete the workspace.

## Document permissions

Editors can upload, replace, and delete documents. Viewers can search documents and read cited answers but cannot change the document library. Document visibility follows workspace membership; per-document sharing is not supported.

## Member removal

Removing a member revokes their interactive sessions immediately. API tokens belonging to a removed member are revoked at the same time. Historical audit records retain the member identifier.

## Invitations

Workspace invitations expire after 72 hours. Only Owners and Admins can invite new members. An expired invitation must be reissued rather than extended.
