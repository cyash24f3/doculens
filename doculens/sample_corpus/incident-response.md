# Incident response

Fictional Atlas documentation for DocuLens evaluation. This is not a real service policy.

## Severity

A SEV1 incident is a complete service outage or a confirmed exposure of customer data. A SEV2 incident is a major feature failure affecting multiple customers. A minor cosmetic defect does not qualify as SEV1.

## Initial response

For a SEV1 incident, the on-call engineer acknowledges the page within 10 minutes. The incident commander opens a dedicated incident channel and assigns an owner to mitigation. A customer-facing update is published within 30 minutes of confirmation.

## Communication cadence

During an active SEV1 incident, the incident commander posts a status update every 30 minutes. The update must state current impact, mitigation progress, and the next update time. Avoid assigning blame while the incident is active.

## Postmortem

A written postmortem is due within 5 business days after a SEV1 incident is resolved. It records the timeline, contributing factors, customer impact, and assigned follow-up actions. Each action requires an owner and a target date.
