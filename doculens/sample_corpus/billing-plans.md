# Plans & billing

Fictional Atlas documentation for DocuLens evaluation. This is not a real service policy.

## Sandbox plan

The fictional Sandbox plan supports 10 members and 100 documents. It is intended for evaluation and has no contractual availability guarantee. No payment information is required to create a Sandbox workspace.

## Team plan

The fictional Team plan supports 50 members and 5,000 documents. Billing is monthly, and invoices are generated on the first day of each billing period. The documentation does not specify a monetary price.

## Plan changes

Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing period. If current usage exceeds the lower plan limit, an Admin must reduce usage before the downgrade can complete.

## Billing contacts

Only Owners can change the billing contact or payment method. Billing contact changes affect future invoices and do not rewrite historical invoices. No credit-card data appears in audit exports.
